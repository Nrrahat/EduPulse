## Algebra

### Question 1
$x, y$ are two real numbers in which their summation is $\sqrt{5}$ and subtraction is $\sqrt{3}$.

* **a.** Find the value of $\sqrt{3}$. *(2)*
* **b.** Prove that, $xy(x^{2}+y^{2})=2$ *(4)*
* **c.** If $a=2x$, then find the value of $a^3 - \frac{1}{a^3}$. *(4)*

### Question 2
$\frac{4}{a}$ and $\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}$ are two expressions.

* **a.** If $1\text{st expression} = 2\text{nd expression}$, show that $a = \frac{4xy}{x+y}$. *(2)*
* **b.** Prove that, $\frac{a+2x}{a-2x}+\frac{a+2y}{a-2y}=2$ from obtained value 'a'. *(4)*
* **c.** If $\frac{a+x}{a-x}+\frac{a+y}{a-y}=2$, then show that, $2\text{nd expression} = \frac{2}{a}$. *(4)*

---

### Question 3

* **a.** Express the information by equation. *(2)*

* **c.** Find the sum of first $10\text{th}$ terms of the series. *(4)*
